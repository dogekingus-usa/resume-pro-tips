ATS Resume Checklist

The exact formatting rules that pass automated screeners — from the ResumeProTips team.

Formatting (passes the machine)

  • ☐ One page (2 max for 10+ years experience)
  • ☐ Standard fonts only: Arial, Calibri, Helvetica, Times New Roman (10-12pt)
  • ☐ No tables, text boxes, columns, graphics, or images
  • ☐ Single-column layout, left-aligned
  • ☐ Standard section headers: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills
  • ☐ Save as .docx or .pdf (docx preferred by most ATS)
  • ☐ No headers/footers with contact info (some ATS miss them)
  • ☐ Bullet points, not paragraphs, under each role

Keyword optimization (15+ tips)

  • ☐ Mirror exact job-posting language — use the same verbs and nouns
  • ☐ Include role-specific skills verbatim (e.g., "accounts payable" not "AP")
  • ☐ Tech roles: list tools/languages in a dedicated Skills section
  • ☐ Use standard job titles, not creative variations
  • ☐ Sprinkle keywords from the posting throughout, not just skills section
  • ☐ Include industry certifications by full name + acronym
  • ☐ Avoid keyword stuffing — natural density, ~2-4% max
  • ☐ Use both spelled-out and abbreviated versions of key terms
  • ☐ Match seniority terms (Senior, Lead, Manager) to the posting
  • ☐ Add soft skills the posting names (leadership, cross-functional)
  • ☐ Include location keywords if the job is location-specific
  • ☐ Education: degree type, field, school name, graduation year
  • ☐ Metrics: numbers beat adjectives ("cut costs 20%" vs "reduced costs")
  • ☐ Action verbs: achieved, launched, led, built, improved, delivered
  • ☐ Reverse-chronological order (most ATS expect newest first)

Common ATS pitfalls (avoid these)

  • ❌ Images/logos/icons in the resume body
  • ❌ Creative fonts or text effects
  • ❌ Contact info inside a table or graphic
  • ❌ Abbreviations the ATS doesn't recognize
  • ❌ More than 2 pages (truncation risk)
  • ❌ .png/.jpg file formats (must be .docx/.pdf)
  • ❌ Missing dates on roles (format consistently: MM/YYYY)
  • ❌ Job titles that don't match the posting language
