How to Create a Skills Section That Passes ATS Every Time

Published: May 15, 2026 | Reading time: 4 min

To create a resume that not only stands out but also passes through Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), crafting an effective skills section is crucial. ATS systems are designed to filter resumes based on specific keywords and criteria, making your skills section a key component in ensuring your application gets noticed by hiring managers. Here's how you can optimize your skills section for ATS success while keeping it impactful.

Understand the Role of an ATS

Applicant Tracking Systems are software tools used by companies to automate the recruitment process, from job posting to candidate screening. They use natural language processing and keyword matching to screen resumes based on specific criteria. By understanding how ATS systems work, you can tailor your skills section more effectively.

  • Keyword Matching: ATS systems look for specific keywords related to the job description. Including these terms in your skills section can significantly increase your chances of passing through the initial screening stage.
  • Structure Matters: A well-structured skills section is easier for ATS systems to read and process, which means your resume might be more likely to pass through without getting flagged as non-compliant or unqualified.

Select Relevant Skills Carefully

Your skills section should highlight the most relevant competencies that align with the job you are applying for. This involves a bit of research and strategic selection, but it's worth it in terms of your resume's visibility to hiring managers.

  • Job Description Analysis: Carefully read through the job description and underline or highlight keywords that relate to skills and experience required. Use these as your guide for selecting which skills to include in your resume.
  • Prioritize Hard & Soft Skills: Ensure a mix of hard (technical) and soft (interpersonal) skills relevant to the position. For example, if you're applying for a sales role, highlight both technical proficiency with CRM software and interpersonal skills like communication and negotiation.

Format Your Skills Section Effectively

The format of your skills section can significantly impact its readability by ATS systems as well as the overall impression it leaves on hiring managers. Here are some tips to ensure your skills section is both effective and ATS-friendly:

  • Bullet Points: List your skills in bullet points for clarity and ease of reading. This format is easier for ATS systems to parse compared to long, paragraph-style descriptions.
  • Clear Separation: Use clear headings or categories (e.g., Technical Skills, Professional Skills) to separate different types of skills. This helps both ATS systems and human readers quickly find the information they need.

Use Keywords Strategically

To maximize your resume's visibility in ATS systems, strategically include keywords from the job description throughout your skills section. However, ensure that these additions are natural and add value to your resume rather than just padding it with irrelevant terms.

  • Job Description Adaptation: Modify generic skill descriptions based on the specific requirements of the job. For example, if a job description mentions "project management," you might include a more detailed version like "Project Management Professional (PMP) certified and experienced in Agile methodologies."
  • Avoid Keyword Stuffing: While including relevant keywords is important, avoid keyword stuffing or unnatural phrasing that could make your resume sound robotic. Balance is key.

Conclusion: Your Skills Section for ATS Success

Crafting an effective skills section that passes through ATS systems every time requires a blend of strategic planning and practical execution. By understanding how ATS systems work, selecting relevant and impactful skills, formatting your section effectively, and using keywords strategically, you can increase the chances of making it past the initial screening stage.

Remember, while an optimized skills section is crucial for ATS success, it's equally important to tailor other sections of your resume (like the summary or experience) to align with the job description. A well-rounded approach will help ensure that your resume stands out not only to ATS systems but also to hiring managers who are looking for a strong fit.
