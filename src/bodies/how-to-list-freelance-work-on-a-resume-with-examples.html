How to List Freelance Work on a Resume (With Examples)

Published: May 14, 2026 | Reading time: 4 min

Introduction to Listing Freelance Work on Your Resume

Freelancing is more than just a job--it's an opportunity to showcase your versatility and expertise in various industries. However, many wonder how to incorporate this freelance experience into their resumes without cluttering the page or making it look unprofessional. This guide will help you present your freelance work effectively by providing actionable tips and examples.

Choosing the Right Format

To make your resume stand out, consider using a combination of chronological and functional sections to highlight both full-time roles and freelance projects. Here's how you can structure it:

  • Chronological Section: List recent or significant freelance work first under relevant job experience.
  • Functional Section: Include a section for skills, where you can list your freelancing specialties and tools used.

Highlighting Your Freelance Projects

Mentioning your freelance projects effectively involves providing specific details that demonstrate your value. Here's how to do it:

  • Include Project Titles: Use clear, concise titles that reflect the nature of your work.
  • List Key Responsibilities: Briefly describe what you did and what value you brought to the project or client.
  • Mention Client Names (Optional): Including reputable clients can enhance credibility. However, always seek permission before listing any names.
  • Include Dates and Duration: Provide a clear timeline for each freelance project.

Example of Freelance Work on Your Resume

Let's take an example to see how you can effectively list your freelance work. Imagine you are a graphic designer who has worked with several clients in the past year:

<ul>
    <li>Freelance Graphic Designer, [Client Name], June 2023 - Present
        <ul>
            <li>Created brand identity materials for a tech startup.</li>
            <li>Designed marketing collaterals for social media campaigns.</li>
        </ul>
    </li>
</ul>

Adding Skills and Achievements

In addition to listing your freelance projects, make sure to highlight the skills you've developed. This can be done in a separate section:

  • Graphic Design Skills: Proficient in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator), InDesign.
  • Communication Skills: Excellent verbal and written communication with clients to understand their needs.

Final Tips for Listing Freelance Work

To ensure your freelance work is presented effectively on your resume:

  • Avoid Overloading Your Resume: Keep the details concise and relevant. Be selective about which projects to include.
  • Use Action Verbs: Start each bullet point with a strong verb like "Created," "Designed," or "Managed."
  • Quantify Results: Where possible, provide quantifiable results such as "Increased website traffic by 30%" to show impact.

By following these tips and examples, you can effectively showcase your freelance experience on your resume. Remember, the goal is not only to list projects but also to convey how they reflect your unique value proposition to potential employers or clients.
