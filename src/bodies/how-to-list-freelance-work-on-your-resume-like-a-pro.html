How to List Freelance Work on Your Resume Like a Pro

Published: May 15, 2026 | Reading time: 4 min

When applying for jobs in today's competitive job market, having a diverse skill set is essential. Freelance work can significantly enhance your resume by showcasing your versatility and adaptability. However, listing freelance experience effectively requires strategic approaches to ensure it highlights your strengths without overshadowing other professional achievements. Here are some tips on how to list freelance work on your resume like a pro.

1. Choose Relevant Projects

Select projects that align with the job you're applying for. It's not about the quantity but quality and relevance. Highlight projects that demonstrate your expertise in specific skills, tools, or industries relevant to the role you are targeting. This approach ensures that employers see value in your freelance experience.

  • Filter out irrelevant tasks or roles; only include those that add value to your application
  • Mention your most significant achievements from these projects such as client satisfaction rates, project success stories, and any additional revenue generated for clients

2. Structure Your Freelance Experience Clearly

When listing freelance work, it's important to present a structured format that is easy to read and understand at a glance. Use bullet points and concise language to describe your role, responsibilities, tools used, and outcomes. This approach makes your resume more digestible for recruiters and hiring managers.

  • Use a reverse chronological order similar to full-time employment
  • List freelance work under a separate section titled "Freelance Work" or "Self-Employed Projects"
  • Include the duration of each project, client name (if appropriate), and your key responsibilities
  • Note any tools or technologies you used during the projects to showcase technical proficiency

3. Quantify Your Achievements

Employers often look for candidates who can demonstrate their impact on previous clients or employers through numbers. Where possible, include quantifiable results from your freelance work such as increased website traffic, higher conversion rates, or cost savings achieved.

  • Add metrics related to project outcomes like number of completed projects, hours worked per week, and average client satisfaction score
  • Highlight any financial contributions you made through freelancing, e.g., "Earned $X in revenue for XYZ company"
  • Mention how your work led to specific improvements or successes, such as "Increased website traffic by 30% within three months of optimization efforts"

4. Showcase Your Professional Growth

Beyond listing what you did, it's crucial to highlight the growth and development that occurred throughout your freelance career. Emphasize any new skills or knowledge gained through freelancing, including industry trends, software proficiency, and personal branding strategies.

  • Detail how your freelance work contributed to developing new skills or expanding existing ones
  • Mention any certifications or training completed during your freelance period that enhanced your professional capabilities
  • Discuss any challenges faced and overcome while working as a freelancer; this shows resilience and adaptability

5. Provide Contact Information for References

Including references can bolster the credibility of your resume, especially when it comes to freelance work where you might not have direct supervisor feedback available. Providing contact information for professional clients can serve as a strong recommendation.

  • Include at least one reference from each significant client or project
  • Get permission before including their contact details and provide them with a brief introduction about your collaboration
  • Ensure all references are willing to vouch for you professionally; avoid mentioning personal friends or family members as professional references

In conclusion, listing freelance work on your resume effectively requires thoughtful selection of projects, clear structuring of information, emphasis on quantifiable achievements, and showcasing continuous professional growth. By following these tips, you can present a compelling narrative that stands out to potential employers while maintaining focus on your core professional qualifications.
