LinkedIn Headline Examples to Land Your Dream Job

Your LinkedIn headline is the most valuable real estate on your profile. Recruiters see it in search results before they open your profile. A strong headline can increase profile views by 300% or more.

The Formula

Best performing LinkedIn headlines follow this formula: Job Title | Specialization | Key Skill | Value Proposition. For example: Marketing Manager | Demand Generation | SEO & Content Strategy | Driving 200% Pipeline Growth. This tells recruiters exactly who you are, what you do, and what you deliver.

Examples by Role

Software Engineer: Full-Stack Developer | React & Node.js | Building Scalable Web Applications. Sales Professional: Enterprise Account Executive | SaaS Sales | Closing 6-Figure Deals. Financial Analyst: Financial Modeling | Investment Analysis | CFA Level 2 Candidate. Each headline targets specific keywords that recruiters search for.

Tips for Better Headlines

Include your target role title. Add your primary skill or specialization. Include a quantifiable achievement. Use keywords from job descriptions in your industry. Keep it under 220 characters. Test different headlines and track which generates more profile views and recruiter messages.
