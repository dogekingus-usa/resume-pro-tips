Frequently Asked Questions

How many action verbs do I need per bullet point?

One strong action verb per bullet point is ideal. Lead with the verb, then follow with context and quantification. Using more than one verb per bullet can make the sentence feel crowded.

Should I use the same keywords for every application?

No. Tailor your keywords for each job application based on the specific job description. The bundle's keyword lists give you a starting point, but you should always customize your resume for the role.
