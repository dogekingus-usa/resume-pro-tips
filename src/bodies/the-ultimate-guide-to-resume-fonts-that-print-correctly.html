The Ultimate Guide to Resume Fonts That Print Correctly

Published: May 15, 2026 | Reading time: 4 min

When crafting your resume, choosing the right font is crucial to ensure that it looks professional while also being easy to read. However, not all fonts are created equal when it comes to printing. This guide will help you select and use fonts that print correctly, ensuring your document looks its best on paper.

Understanding Font Compatibility

To make sure your resume prints correctly, it's important to understand the compatibility of different font types:

  • TrueType and PostScript Fonts: These are scalable fonts that can be resized without losing quality. They're generally safe for printing as long as they're installed on the computer or printer being used.
  • Web Safe Fonts: Commonly used in web design, these fonts may not always print as intended due to variations between operating systems and printers. Examples include Arial, Times New Roman, and Verdana.
  • Custom and Embedded Fonts: These are specific to the document and can sometimes cause issues if they aren't embedded properly or if the recipient doesn't have them installed on their computer.

Selecting Professional Fonts

To keep your resume looking professional, choose fonts that convey seriousness and clarity. Here are some popular options:

  • Arial: A clean sans-serif font that is widely available on most computers.
  • Courier New: Ideal for resumes requiring a more traditional, typewritten look.
  • Garamond or Times New Roman: Serif fonts that are easy to read in printed form and add a classic touch.

    Avoiding Common Mistakes

    To ensure your resume prints beautifully, avoid these common pitfalls:

    • Using Too Many Font Styles: Stick to one or two font families. Mixing too many styles can make the document look cluttered.
    • Inconsistent Spacing and Margins: Ensure that all text is properly aligned with consistent spacing between lines and paragraphs.
    • Overusing Bold or Italic Text: Reserve bolding for headings or key points, but avoid overuse as it can make the document look unprofessional.

      Printing Tips

      To guarantee your resume looks its best on paper, follow these printing tips:

      • Check Font Embedding Settings: Ensure that all fonts are properly embedded in the document. This can usually be done through a print preview or by checking with your word processor's settings.
      • Use High-Quality Paper: Choose matte or high-quality paper for better printing results, as glossy paper may cause text to become smudged.

        Conclusion

        Selecting the right fonts is just one aspect of creating an effective resume. By choosing professional and compatible fonts, avoiding common mistakes, and following proper printing guidelines, you can ensure that your document looks polished and easy to read in both digital and printed forms.
