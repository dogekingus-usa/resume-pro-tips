Top 10 ATS Keywords to Include in Your Resume Right Now

In today's digital job market, Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) play a crucial role in filtering and sorting through thousands of resumes. To ensure your resume gets past these systems and lands in the hands of hiring managers, it's essential to include specific keywords that ATS look for. Here are the top 10 ATS keywords you should consider adding to boost your chances of being noticed.

Technical Skills and Tools

To stand out, highlight the technical skills and tools relevant to the job you're applying for. These can be software programs, programming languages, or hardware technologies that are commonly used in your industry.

  • Programming Languages: Python, Java, C++, JavaScript
  • Software Tools: Microsoft Office Suite, Adobe Creative Suite, Salesforce CRM
  • Project Management: Agile, Scrum, Kanban
  • Databases and Data Analysis: SQL, R, Tableau, PowerBI

Job Responsibilities and Achievements

Include specific job responsibilities and achievements that align with the requirements of the position. This not only shows your past experience but also demonstrates how you can contribute to the company's goals.

  • Responsibilities: Managed a team of 10+ developers, led project from conception to launch
  • Achievements: Increased website traffic by 30% in six months through SEO optimization, saved $50,000 on operational costs within one year

Education and Certifications

Mention any relevant education or certifications that align with the job. This can include degree names, specialization areas, or professional certifications.

  • Degree Names: Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, Master's in Business Administration (MBA)
  • Certifications: Certified Public Accountant (CPA), Project Management Professional (PMP), AWS Certified Solutions Architect

Languages and Soft Skills

Including a section for languages or soft skills can also help your resume pass ATS. Highlighting multilingual abilities and core competencies like communication, teamwork, and leadership will give you an edge.

  • Languages: Fluent in Spanish, German
  • Soft Skills: Excellent written and verbal communication skills, strong problem-solving ability, collaborative team player

By carefully selecting the right keywords for your resume, you can significantly improve its chances of being noticed by both ATS systems and human reviewers. Remember to tailor your resume to each application, ensuring that it accurately reflects your experience, skills, and achievements relevant to the job.
